## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a visceral, high-stakes underground hip-hop arena. Built for freestyle battles, live cyphers, and decisive crowd voting, the aesthetic marries gritty street realism with high-voltage neon energy. It communicates raw talent, unfiltered competition, and real-time urgency.

### Aesthetic Influences
- **Industrial Brutalism meets Cyber-Street:** Matte graphite surfaces, distressed concrete overlays, clipped 45-degree chamfers, and razor-sharp borders.
- **Neon Glow & High Contrast:** Piercing acid lime, ember neon orange, and warning-level red against deep subterranean slates.
- **Battle Poster Ephemera:** Stencil-like stamps, oversized uppercase typography, high-contrast duo-tone fighter imagery, and aggressive typographic stacking that evokes warehouse event flyers and classic cassette mixtape tapes.

## Colors

The palette operates strictly in dark mode, reflecting the atmosphere of dim, sweat-soaked underground venues lit only by tube lighting and stage strobes.

- **Primary (`#D4FF00` - Acid Lime):** The core action color. Used for active voting states, winner badges, live audio waveforms, and primary battle status calls.
- **Secondary (`#FF5500` - Blazing Neon Orange):** High-heat secondary accent for competitor vs. competitor callouts, round indicators, and burning hype meters.
- **Tertiary (`#FF1A35` - Electric Battle Red):** Critical indicators, countdown timers, sudden death sudden-elimination states, and voting lockouts.
- **Neutral Core (`#0D0E11` - Sub-Bass Charcoal):** Deep slate-black canvas that prevents pure black eye fatigue while giving neon elements maximum luminous punch.
  - **Surface Elevated (`#16181D`):** Card backings, bracket nodes, and participant roster panels.
  - **Surface Highlight (`#22262F`):** Hover states, active borders, and textured framing.
  - **Text Neutral Primary (`#F4F6F8`):** Off-white newsprint tone for maximum legibility against dark slate.
  - **Text Neutral Muted (`#8A92A0`):** Secondary metadata, stats, and timestamp readouts.

## Typography

The typography pairs monumental poster impact with technical accuracy:

- **Headlines (`Bebas Neue`):** All headline levels render strictly in uppercase with slight tracking expansion. Evokes traditional wooden letterpress fight cards, heavyweight bout billings, and tournament flyposters.
- **Body & Editorial (`Space Grotesk`):** Angular, tech-forward, and legible even under high contrast and compression. Used for competitor bios, judging criteria descriptions, and live spectator chat.
- **Data & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Monospaced precision for punch counts, live voting percentages, time clocks, decibel levels, and bracket seeded numbers. Always tracked out for tactical readability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a tight 4px baseline sub-grid supporting a structural 12-column grid for arena-scale desktop dashboards, and a high-density 4-column layout for mobile cypher viewers.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max layout bounds of 1440px. 12 columns, 24px gutters, and 48px page padding. Allows side-by-side MC dual battle screens with center-placed live voting telemetry and bracket overlays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Brackets switch into horizontally scrollable lanes with persistent floating vote locks.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns, 12px gutters, 16px margins. Heavy emphasis on full-bleed vertical split cards where Top MC vs. Bottom MC fill screen real estate for thumb-driven live scoring.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft Gaussian blurs or natural lighting. Depth is forged using layered dark surfaces, razor wire borders, and electric radiance:

- **Tier 0 (The Trench):** Background canvas (`#0D0E11`) with faint repeating SVG gridlines and industrial noise textures.
- **Tier 1 (Bunkers & Cards):** Raised containers (`#16181D`) bordered by a 1px structural stroke of `#22262F`. 
- **Tier 2 (The Stage / Active Focus):** Fighter poster cards in active rounds receive an outer 2px hard border in `#D4FF00` alongside an intense phosphor glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.45)`).
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Semi-opaque backdrop blackout (`rgba(5, 6, 8, 0.88)`) with backdrop blur of 8px, framed by high-contrast neon corner marks.

## Shapes

The design system is uncompromisingly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Corners:** 0px radius across buttons, cards, dialogs, and tags. Everything has hard, chiseled architectural edges.
- **Chamfered Cuts:** Key battle components (such as "VOTE NOW" pushers and bracket nodes) utilize 45-degree diagonal corner clips (`clip-path: polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 10px) 0, 100% 10px, 100% 100%, 0 100%)`) to echo military-spec stencils and reinforced touring flight cases.
- **Divider Rules:** Solid, scored lines (1px to 3px) interrupted by crosshair indicators or coordinate markers (`+`, `—`).

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Tactile Voting Triggers
- **Cast Vote Slam Button:** Solid Acid Lime (`#D4FF00`) block with pitch-black label in `Bebas Neue` (24px). Interactive states feature an inverted 2px inset displacement on active press with an immediate neon bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 32px rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.7)`).
- **Secondary Action Button:** Charcoal fill with a 1px border of `#8A92A0`, uppercase `JetBrains Mono` label. On hover, border shifts to Orange (`#FF5500`) with text glow.
- **Lock-in Vote State:** Pulsing warning red borders with a monospaced countdown clock ticking hundredths of a second.

### 2. Battle Poster Cards
- Split-screen or dual-card layout for MC matchups.
- Features high-contrast desaturated/duo-tone photography with grain overlay. Silhouetted avatars feature a harsh back-light rim in either Lime (Fighter A) or Orange (Fighter B).
- Bottom edge houses real-time crowd meter gauges: horizontal segmented progress bars styled like vintage LED audio level meters (VU meters).

### 3. Tournament Brackets
- Orthogonal routing lines with 2px width in muted slate `#22262F`, snapping to bright neon when an MC advances.
- Participant nodes contain the seed number (`#8A92A0`), MC stage name in condensed uppercase, and current round score. Eliminated participants are instantly crossed out with a diagonal strike and reduced to 35% opacity.

### 4. Chips, Badges & Tags
- Rectangular, zero-radius pills.
- **Live Now Badge:** Solid Red (`#FF1A35`) background with blinking white recording dot and monospaced text: `[REC ● LIVE]`.
- **Weight Class / Region Tag:** Dark slate with thin wireframe borders and technical coordinates (e.g., `DIV // HEAVYWEIGHT - EAST COAST`).

### 5. Inputs & Chat Feed
- **Live Crowd Chat / Bars Input:** Terminal-style single-line input field. Black interior, 1px bottom and side borders, active state reveals an acid-green blinking block cursor.
- **Chat Rows:** Stripped-down layout: monospaced user handles in neon accents followed by raw white chat remarks, auto-scrolling with micro-haptic visual bump animations.
